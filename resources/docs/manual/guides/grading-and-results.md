## What is marked automatically

Multiple choice, multiple select, true/false, matching, ordering, fill in the blank and short answers with a set answer are **marked instantly** when the student submits. Essays, file uploads, code submissions and assignments need **you** to give the score.

All scores are shown as a **percentage of the points available**. A student with 3 of 4 points sees **75%**.

## Where to find submissions

- **Submissions** in the sidebar (under **Teaching**, or **Oversight** for supervisors) lists everything waiting for marking across your courses. The number next to it is how many are waiting.
- Inside an assessment, the **Submissions** tab shows **Submitted**, **Needs grading**, **Average score** and **Pass rate**, then one row per student. **Awaiting grade** marks attempts you still need to score.

## Grade a submission

![The Grade This Submission panel](manual:grading-panel.png)

1. Click **Grade** (or **View**) next to the student on the Submissions tab, or open them from the Submissions page.
2. The results page shows the student's score at the top. Below that is the **Grade This Submission** panel:
   - **Auto-graded**: points already earned on automatic questions.
   - **Needs your score**: how many questions you must mark.
   - **Full answer review**: every question with the student's answer, the correct answer, and **Grade manually** labels.
3. Click **Enter Final Grade** (or **Update Grade**), type the final score out of the maximum and optional **Feedback**.
4. Click **Save Grade & Notify Student**. The student's result, pass/fail and XP update, and they are notified.

> The suggested starting score is the auto-graded total. Add points for the open answers on top of it.

## Why old results don't change

Every attempt keeps a frozen copy of the questions and answers the student actually saw. Editing or deleting a question later never changes past scores or what the review shows.
