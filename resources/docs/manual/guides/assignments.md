An **assignment** is an assessment where students hand in work, such as text or files, instead of answering questions.

## Create an assignment

1. Open **Assignments** and click **New Assignment**, or add one to a lesson from the Curriculum builder.
2. In the assessment editor's **Settings** tab, fill in **Assignment details**:
   - **Instructions**
   - **Due date**
   - **Max points**
   - what students hand in: **Text response** and/or **File upload**
   - optional **Brief files** for students to download
3. Click **Save changes** and make sure it's **Open to students**.

## Collect and grade work

Open **Submissions** in the sidebar:

1. The cards show **Total Submissions**, **Pending Grading**, **Graded** and **Overdue**.
2. Filter by status, submission type, course or camp.
3. Click **View** on a submission to read the text or download the files.
4. Click **Grade Submission** (or **Edit Grade**), enter the **Score** and **Feedback**, and click **Save Grade**.

The student is notified and sees your score and feedback on their results page.

> Quizzes with essay or file-upload questions show up here too. See *Results, grading and submissions*.
