Daily reports, club session reports, student submissions and revised content all have a **Comments** section at the bottom. Use it to give feedback or ask a question instead of sending a separate message.

## Leave a comment

1. Open the report or submission.
2. Scroll to **Comments**, type your message and click **Post comment**.

![The Comments section at the bottom of a daily report](manual:comments-thread.png)

Whoever wrote the report or submission is notified. When they reply, everyone else in the conversation is notified. You are never notified about your own comments. The writer's comments are marked **Author**.

To delete one of your comments, hover over it and click the bin icon. Admins can delete any comment.

## Where to find comments

- **Trainers:** open **Daily Reports**. Under the form, **My recent reports** lists your reports, with a speech-bubble count on any that have comments. Click one to read and reply.
- **Club facilitators:** open **My Session Reports** and click a report.
- **Admins and supervisors:**
  - **Reports → Daily Reports**: a speech-bubble count shows reports with comments. Click **View** to open one.
  - **Reports → Club Session Reports**: click a report to open its full page, then **Mark reviewed** when you're done.
  - **Revised Content** and student **Submissions** have the same Comments section.

Comment notifications appear under the bell icon, with a link back to the conversation.
