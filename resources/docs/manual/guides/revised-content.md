When you improve teaching materials during a camp, send them in so the next camp uses the better version.

## Trainers: submit revised content

1. Open **Revised Content** in the sidebar and click **Submit revised content**.
2. Choose the **Camp**. Optionally choose the **Course it replaces or improves**.
3. Give it a **Title**, for example *Week 2 Python slides, reworked with more practice*.
4. In **What changed and why**, explain what you changed, what students struggled with and what worked better.
5. Under **Files**, click **Choose files**. You can add slides, documents, Scratch projects (.sb3), Python files, zip folders and images: up to 15 files, 12 MB each.
6. Click **Send to supervisor**.

![The Submit revised content form](manual:revised-content-form.png)

Supervisors and admins are notified. You are notified when it is approved or sent back.

## Supervisors and admins: review it

1. Open **Reports → Revised Content**. The number on the menu item shows how many are waiting.
2. Open a submission and download the files.
3. Add a note if you like, then click **Approve** or **Request changes**.
4. Use **Comments** at the bottom to discuss it with the trainer.

Revised content also appears in the *End-of-camp report*, under *Student feedback and issues*.
