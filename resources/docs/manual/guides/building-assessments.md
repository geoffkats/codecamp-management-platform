Every quiz, test and assignment has one editor with three tabs: **Questions**, **Settings** and **Submissions**. You can reach it from **Teaching → Assessments**, or from the lesson inside the **Curriculum** builder. Both open the same editor.

![The assessment editor showing the Questions tab](manual:assessment-questions-tab.png)

## 1. Add questions

On the **Questions** tab:

1. Click **New question** to write a question just for this assessment, or
2. Click **From Question Bank** to reuse questions you or other trainers already wrote (see *Using the question bank*).
3. Each question card shows its type, points and difficulty, with the correct answers in green. Use the arrows to reorder, **Edit** to change it, or the bin icon to remove it from this assessment.

> **Shared questions:** A question can be used in several assessments. Editing it changes it everywhere, but students who already **started** an attempt keep the version they were given.

## 2. Random question pool (optional)

With two or more questions, a **random pool** panel appears above the list.

![The random pool panel: each student gets 3 of 5 questions](manual:random-pool.png)

1. In **Per attempt**, type how many questions each student should get (for example 10 of 25).
2. Click **Use random pool**.
3. Every attempt now draws a fresh random set in a random order, so students can't copy each other or memorise the paper. Click **Use all questions** to switch back.

## 3. Settings

![The Settings tab with Basics and Rules](manual:assessment-settings-tab.png)

On the **Settings** tab:

- **Basics:** title and description (shown to students before they start). The type (Quiz, Assignment, Pre-project test, and so on) can't be changed after creation.
- **Rules:** **Attempts**, **Time limit (min)** (leave empty for no limit), **Pass mark %** and **XP reward**.
- **Switches:**
  - **Shuffle question order** and **Shuffle answers**.
  - **Show score right away** lets students see their result as soon as they submit.
  - **Show correct answers** and **Allow review** control what students can see afterwards.
  - **Required** means students must complete it to finish the lesson.
- Assignments get an **Assignment details** section instead: instructions, due date, max points, what students can hand in (text and/or files), and brief files.

Click **Save changes** at the bottom. Changes apply to **new** attempts. Attempts already started keep their questions.

## 4. Open or lock it

The button at the top right shows **Open to students** or **Locked**. Click it to switch. A locked assessment can't be opened by students. That's useful while you are still writing it, or to close it after the deadline.

## 5. Watch the submissions

The **Submissions** tab shows how many students submitted, how many **Need grading**, the **Average score** and the **Pass rate**, then one row per student. See *Results, grading and submissions*.
