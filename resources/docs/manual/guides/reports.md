## Daily class report (Code Camp trainers)

After class, open **Daily Reports**, or click **Submit report** on the reminder banner. **Not today** hides the banner. The report is optional, but it helps your supervisor.

1. Check the **Date** and **Camp**, and choose the **Course** (required).
2. Fill in:
   - **What was covered today?**
   - **Challenges**
   - **General issues / notes**
   - **Pedagogical approaches applied today**
3. **Attendance** is filled in from the students' check-ins. Correct it if needed.
4. Add **Student Highlights**, **Flagged Issues** and any **Attachments** (photos, files).
5. Click **Submit Report**.

## Club session report (Code Club facilitators)

Open **Session Reports** after each club session:

1. The required fields are **Session Date**, **Club**, **Attendance Count** and **Summary**.
2. Optional fields: **Topics Taught**, **New Techniques Introduced** and **Challenges**.
3. Rate **Teamwork** and **Collaboration** from 1 to 5 stars.
4. Tick **Follow-up required** if someone needs to act on something.
5. Click **Submit Report**.

## Reading reports (admins and supervisors)

- **Reports → Daily Reports**: filter by camp, course, instructor and status (**Submitted** / **Draft**).
- **Reports → Club Session Reports**: open a report, click **Mark reviewed**, add notes and **Confirm**. Reports marked **Follow-up required** need attention first.
