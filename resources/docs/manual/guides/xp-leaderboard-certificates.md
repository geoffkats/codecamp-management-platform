## Award XP

Open **Award XP** (admins and supervisors: **XP Manager**).

1. Click **Award XP** on a student's row.
2. Choose the **Course**, the **Action** (**Add XP** or **Remove XP**), the number of **Points**, and a **Reason** (optional, but it helps when someone asks later).
3. Click **Award to this course**.

**For a whole class:** filter by course and click **Award all students in this course**, or tick some students and use **Apply to course**.

> A student must be **enrolled in the course** to get XP for it. Trainers can only award XP in their own courses.

Admins and supervisors also have **Sync All Levels**, **Reset Week** and **Reset All XP**. Use them with care: resets can't be undone.

Students also earn XP automatically for passing quizzes (the **XP reward** in each assessment's Settings tab).

## Leaderboard

**Leaderboard** ranks students by **XP** or **Level**, for **All Time**, **This Month** or **This Week**. Filter by camp, club or course, and click **Reset** to clear the filters.

## Issue certificates

Open **Certificates** (the certificate centre):

1. **Ready to issue** lists students who finished a course or reached the minimum progress set by an admin.
2. Tick the students.
3. Choose **Add to existing certificate** (one certificate listing several courses) or **Separate per course**.
4. Pick the signatory and whether to **Download PDFs**.
5. Click **Issue N certificates**.

Other tabs: **Issued** (past certificates, re-issue), **One student** and **CSV import**.
