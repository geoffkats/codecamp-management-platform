Open **Teaching → Curriculum**. The builder has your course list on the left and the selected lesson or module on the right.

## 1. Pick a course

1. In the left panel under **My Courses**, click the course. Courses you share with another trainer show a **Collab** badge.
2. No courses yet? Click **Create a Course**.

## 2. Add a module

1. Click **Add Module**.
2. Type the **Module Title** (required) and an optional **Description**.
3. Click **Create Module**.

## 3. Add a lesson

1. Under the module, click **+ Add Lesson**. The **Add Lesson** button at the top only works after you select a module.
2. Fill in the lesson title, choose the **Module**, and pick a **Lesson Type**: **Text**, **Video**, **Interactive** or **Quiz**.
3. Add the lesson content.
4. Click **Create Lesson**.

> **Trainers:** new and edited lessons are sent to an admin or supervisor for approval automatically. You'll see *"Lesson created and sent for approval!"*. An amber dot means **Pending** and a red dot means **Rejected**. See *Content approval*.

## 4. Add a quiz to a lesson

1. Save the lesson first. Until then you'll see *"Save the lesson first, then you can add quizzes."*
2. In **Quizzes & Assessments**, click **Add Quiz**.
3. Click **Create New Assessment**. The assessment editor opens, where you add questions (see *Building a quiz or assessment*).
4. Back in the lesson, each quiz shows **Edit** and **Remove**, and a switch reading **Unlocked – Click to Lock** / **Locked – Click to Unlock**.

## 5. Reorder

Hover over a module or lesson to see the grip handle, then drag it. The new order saves straight away.

## 6. Publish

- **A lesson:** open **Advanced Settings** → **Visibility**:
  - **Published (visible to students)**
  - **Free Preview (visible without enrolment)**
  - **Locked (requires prerequisite)**

  Then save. New lessons start **unpublished**.
- **The whole course:** open **Course Settings** → **Publication Status** and click **Publish** / **Unpublish**.

## 7. Archive and restore

Use the bin icon, **Archive lesson** or **Archive Module**. Nothing is deleted. Open the **Archived** tab and click **Restore** to bring it back.
