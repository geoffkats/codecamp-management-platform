## Add a student

1. Open **Students** in the sidebar and click **Add student**.
2. Choose the programme: **ICT / ICDL Student**, **Code Club Student** or **CodeCamp Student**.
3. Fill in the form:
   - **CodeCamp:** sections A–E. Section E needs **Email**, **Password** and **Confirm Password**. Click **Create Student Record**.
   - **Code Club:** leave Student ID and password blank to have them generated. Click **Register Student**.
   - **ICT:** the password is optional. Click **Save Student**.
4. To change details later, click the pencil icon on the student's row.

**Uniforms (CodeCamp):** in **Section D — Uniform & Fees**, each uniform has its own **Size** and **Paid** tick. Click **+ Add another uniform** for students who buy more than one (up to 10). The heading shows how many are paid, for example *1 of 2 paid*.

![Two uniforms on one student, one paid](manual:uniforms.png)

Code Club facilitators can add a whole class at once with **Bulk import**.

## Do things for many students at once

Tick the students. A bar appears at the bottom with:

- **Assign course** (ICT: **Enrol in module**). Pick the course, optionally tick **Notify students about the new course**, and click **Assign**.
- **Set class** (Code Club).
- **Export CSV**, or **Print logins** to hand out usernames and passwords.
- **More** → **Remove from club**, **Deactivate accounts**, **Delete from system**. Not every role sees every option.

## Reset a password (admins)

The Students page has no password reset. Use **People → Users** instead:

1. Find the student (tabs **Staff**, **Students**, **ICT Students**).
2. Open the row menu and choose **Reset password**.
3. The new password is shown **once**. Click **Copy**, give it to the student, then **Done**.

## Enrollments page (admins)

**People → Enrollments** handles course access for many people:

1. Click **Enroll students** and choose **Enroll now** or **Send invitation**.
2. Tick the courses under **1. Courses** and the students under **2. Students**.
3. Click **Enroll students** (or **Send invitations**).
4. The **Requests** tab lists students asking to join a course. Click **Approve** or **Reject**.
