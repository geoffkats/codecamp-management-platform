Everything you can do is in the **sidebar** on the left. The menu is built from your role, so a trainer, a supervisor and an admin each see a different set of sections. If something in this manual is missing from your sidebar, your account doesn't have that permission. Ask an administrator.

![The help tab on the right edge of the screen and the open manual](manual:help-drawer.png)

*The orange "Help & What's new" tab opens this manual from any page.*

## Your role badge

Under the logo at the top of the sidebar there's a small badge: **Administrator**, **Supervisor**, **ICT Teacher**, **Club Facilitator**, **Teacher**, **Operations** or **Student**. It shows which menus the system is giving you.

## This help drawer

1. Click the orange **Help & What's new** tab on the right edge of any page.
2. **What's new** lists recent changes that affect your work. A green dot on the tab means there's something you haven't read yet.
3. **Guides** are step-by-step instructions grouped by topic.
4. Type in the search box to find a guide, e.g. *grading*, *Scratch* or *attendance*.
5. Use the expand icon at the top to open the manual as a full page. **Copy link to this guide** at the bottom of each guide lets you share it with a colleague.

> **Tip:** Press <kbd>Esc</kbd> to close the drawer.

## Program switcher

Trainers who work in both Code Camp and Code Club see a **program switcher** above the menu. The menu and dashboard change to match the program you pick.
