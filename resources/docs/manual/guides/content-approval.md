Lessons written by trainers are checked by an admin or supervisor before students see them.

## For trainers

- Creating or editing a lesson in the Curriculum builder sends it for approval automatically. Draft lessons show a **Submit for Approval** button.
- Status dots in the builder: **amber = Pending**, **red = Rejected**. Open a rejected lesson to read the reason, fix it and save. It goes back to Pending.
- When it's approved you get a **Content Approved** notification and the lesson is published.

## For admins and supervisors

Open **Content Approval** in the sidebar. The number next to it is how many items are waiting.

1. The cards at the top show **Pending**, **Approved**, **Rejected** and **Total**. Filter by **Status**, **Type** (Courses, Lessons, Modules, Assessments) and **Priority**.
2. Click an item to open **Content Review** and read it.
3. Add **Review Notes** (optional), then:
   - **Approve**: the lesson is published and the trainer is notified.
   - **Reject**: type a **Rejection Reason** so the trainer knows what to fix. They are notified.

> **Tip:** the quick **Approve** and **Reject** buttons on the list are faster but less complete. Quick approve doesn't publish the lesson or notify the trainer, and quick reject saves the reason as just *"Needs revision"*. Use **Content Review** when the trainer needs feedback.

When nothing is waiting you'll see **All Clear!**
