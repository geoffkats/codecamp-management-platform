## Internal test marks (ICT teachers)

Open **Test Marks** → **Enter Internal Test Marks**:

1. Choose the **Student** and the **Course Assigned**.
2. Type the **Test Name**, the **Score (0–100)** and the **Test Date**.
3. **Pass / Fail** fills itself in: **75 or more is a pass**.
4. Add a **Teacher Comment** if useful.
5. Click one of:
   - **Save Marks**
   - **Save & Mark Needs Practice**, for students who should repeat before the exam
   - **Save & Continue**, to enter the next student straight away

Earlier results appear under **Previous Test Attempts**, where you can **Edit** them.

## ICDL exam results

1. Open **ICDL Exam Marks** → **ICDL Exam Result Submission**.
2. Choose the **Exam Attempt** and type the **Score**.
3. Click **Submit for Admin Verification** (or **Submit & Continue**).

The result is then **locked and pending review**. If an admin sends it back, it appears under **Pending Corrections (Unlocked by Admin)**. Correct it and submit again.

## For admins

Under **ICDL → ICDL Exam Marks**, open a result and click **Review**. Then choose:

- **Approve & Lock**, to confirm it,
- **Unlock**, to send it back to the teacher,
- **Save Correction**, to fix it yourself,
- **Reject**.
