## Staff accounts

**People → Users**:

1. Click **Add User**.
2. Fill in **Full name**, **Email** and **Password**.
3. Tick the **Roles**. The role decides which sidebar menu and which help guides the person sees.
4. Leave **Account is active** ticked, then save.

From the row menu you can **Edit**, **Reset password**, **Deactivate** / **Activate** or **Delete**. Deactivating is safer than deleting: the person can't log in, but their history is kept.

## Audit logs: who changed what

**System → Audit Logs** shows **Activity Logs** for every important change.

- Use **Filter** to find an action, a user or a date. **Reset** clears the filters, and **Export CSV** downloads the list.
- Open an entry to see exactly what changed. From there you can restore or revert it.
- **Restore Deleted Items** brings back things that were deleted by mistake.

> **Permanent Delete** really removes data and can't be undone.

## System settings

**System → System Settings** has these sections:

- **Branding**: logo and names.
- **Contact Information**.
- **Certificate**: artwork, colours, **Signatory names**, **Electronic signatures** and the minimum progress for a certificate.
- **Website Registration API**.
- **Google Analytics & Ads**.

Click **Save Settings** at the bottom.
