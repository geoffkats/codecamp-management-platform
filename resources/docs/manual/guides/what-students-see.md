Students take quizzes and tests on a full-screen, game-style page inspired by Kahoot.

![The quiz screen](manual:take-quiz.png)

## The quiz screen

- **Top bar:** quiz title, a **3 / 10** counter and, if there's a time limit, a round **countdown timer**. The ring turns red in the last minute. When time runs out, the quiz is **submitted automatically**.
- **Question card:** the question in large text. Code and Scratch scripts are shown as code panels and blocks.
- **Answer tiles:** big coloured tiles with shapes (red triangle, blue diamond, yellow circle, green square). The chosen tile gets a white outline and a tick, and the others fade. For "select all that apply" questions, students can pick several.
- **True / False:** two big tiles, blue for True and red for False.
- Typed answers (short answer, fill in the blank, code, file upload…) appear in a white answer box.

![A true/false question](manual:take-true-false.png)

## Moving around

- **Next** / **Skip** and **Previous** move between questions.
- The numbered dots at the bottom show every question. Answered ones are brighter, a yellow dot means **bookmarked** and a red dot means **flagged**. Students can jump to any question.
- **Review & Submit** shows every answer on one screen, highlighting anything unanswered, before the final **Confirm & Submit**.
- Keyboard: <kbd>1</kbd>–<kbd>4</kbd> pick an answer, <kbd>←</kbd> <kbd>→</kbd> move between questions.

## Saving

Answers are saved as students go, and a green **Progress saved** message appears. If a student refreshes or loses connection, they come back to the same questions in the same order with their answers kept.

Each student's questions are fixed the moment they start. If you edit a question while they're taking the quiz, their attempt doesn't change.

## After submitting

![The results page](manual:results-page.png)

What the student sees depends on the assessment settings (**Show score right away**, **Show correct answers**, **Allow review**):

- a score ring with **Passed!** or **Not passed yet**, plus tiles for **Correct**, **Wrong** and **Points**,
- **Let's fix these**: each wrong question with *Your answer* (red) next to the *Correct answer* (green),
- **You got these right**,
- **Retake** (if attempts remain and they didn't pass) and **Back to Lesson**.

If the quiz has questions you must mark by hand, the student sees **Waiting for your teacher** until you grade it.
