Every Code Camp has an end-of-camp report. It is built from the camp's attendance, the instructors' daily reports, assessment results, lesson feedback and logged issues. You never type the numbers yourself.

## Open a report

Any of these works:

- **Reports → Camp Reports** lists every camp. Click **Open report**, or **PDF** to download it straight away.
- **Programs → Code Camps**: click **Report** next to a camp.
- On a camp's page, click **End of camp report**.

![Reports → Camp Reports lists every camp with Open report and PDF buttons](manual:camp-reports-list.png)

When a camp is moved to **Completed**, admins and supervisors get a *Camp report ready* notification with a link.

You can open the report while a camp is still running. It shows the figures so far.

## What's in the report

![The top of an end-of-camp report: letterhead, key details, executive summary and enrolment](manual:camp-report-document.png)

| Section | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **1 Executive summary** | A written summary and the key figures: students, attendance, students needing follow-up, daily reports, average score and lesson rating. |
| **2 Enrolment** | Enrolled, active, completed, transferred and dropped students, boys and girls, average age, uniforms paid and students per class. |
| **3 Attendance** | Day-by-day attendance, how many students attended excellently, well, fairly or poorly, attendance by class, and the students below 60% who need follow-up. |
| **4 Teaching and instructor reports** | Reports per instructor, teaching approaches used, **recurring challenges** grouped by theme, and a short summary of what each instructor reported. |
| **5 Learning outcomes** | Students assessed, average score, pass rate, certificates and progress per course. |
| **6 Student feedback and issues** | Lesson ratings, student comments, logged issues and revised content from trainers. |
| **7 Findings and recommendations** | What went well, challenges and recommendations for the next camp. |
| **Appendix A** | The full attendance register for every student. |

The report stays short even for 200 students and hundreds of daily reports:

- Only the students below 60% attendance are named in section 3 (the first 15). Everyone else is in **Appendix A**. On screen, click **Show all students** and use the search box.
- Instructor notes are not printed one by one. All challenges and issues are grouped into themes such as *Laptops and devices* or *Internet and power*, with the number of reports and instructors that raised each one. Click **Read all daily reports for this camp** to see the original reports.

![Section 4.3 groups every challenge from all daily reports into themes](manual:camp-report-themes.png)

## The written summary and findings

The summary and the three findings lists are written for you:

- **Recompile text** writes them from the camp's figures and the grouped challenges.
- **Rewrite with AI** appears instead when AI is set up. It reads the figures and a sample of instructor notes and writes the text in full sentences. If the AI is unavailable, the report falls back to the compiled text and tells you.
- **Edit wording** lets you change any of it. Put one point per line in the lists, then click **Save wording**. The toolbar shows who wrote the current text (*Compiled automatically*, *Written by AI* or *Edited by staff*).

> Rewriting replaces your edits, so make wording changes last.

## Download and share

Click **Download PDF**. The PDF has the same numbered sections, page numbers, your logo, and **Prepared by** and **Reviewed by** lines for signatures. The attendance register starts on a new page at the end.
