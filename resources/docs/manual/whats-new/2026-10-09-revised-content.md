**What changed:** there is a new **Revised Content** page for the improvements trainers make while teaching.

**Trainers:** click **Revised Content → Submit revised content**. Choose the camp, explain what changed and why, attach your files (slides, documents, .sb3, Python, zip, images) and click **Send to supervisor**. You are notified when it is approved or sent back.

**Supervisors and admins:** **Reports → Revised Content** shows how many are waiting. Open one, download the files, then click **Approve** or **Request changes**. Use **Comments** to discuss it with the trainer.
