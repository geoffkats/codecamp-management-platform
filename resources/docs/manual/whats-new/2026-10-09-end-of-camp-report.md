**What changed:** every Code Camp now has an end-of-camp report. Open **Reports → Camp Reports**, or click **Report** next to a camp.

**What's in it:** an executive summary and key figures, enrolment, day-by-day attendance, the students who need follow-up, a summary of every instructor's reports, recurring challenges grouped by theme, learning results, student feedback, issues, and recommendations for the next camp. The full student register is at the end as an appendix.

**Built for big camps:** with 200 students or hundreds of daily reports, the report still reads in a few pages. It shows totals and themes, and names only the students and issues that need action.

**How to use it:**

1. Open the report. The text is written for you from the camp data.
2. Click **Rewrite with AI** (when available) or **Recompile text** to refresh the summary from the latest reports.
3. Click **Edit wording** to adjust anything.
4. Click **Download PDF** to share or print it. It includes signature lines.

You also get a *Camp report ready* notification when a camp is marked **Completed**.
