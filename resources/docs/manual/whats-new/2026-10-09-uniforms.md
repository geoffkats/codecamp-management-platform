**What changed:** the CodeCamp student form used to hold one uniform size. **Section D — Uniform & Fees** now lists each uniform separately.

**How to use it:** choose a **Size** and tick **Paid** for each one. Click **+ Add another uniform** when a student buys more than one, or **Remove** to take one off. The heading shows how many are paid, for example *1 of 2 paid*.

Existing students keep their recorded size and payment. Uniform payments also appear in the end-of-camp report.
