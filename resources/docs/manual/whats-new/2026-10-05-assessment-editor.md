**What changed:** opening a quiz, test or assignment now takes you to a single editor with three tabs:

1. **Questions**: add, reorder, edit and remove questions. Add from the question bank or write new ones.
2. **Settings**: title, attempts, time limit, pass mark, XP, shuffling and what students see afterwards.
3. **Submissions**: who has submitted, what needs grading, the average score and the pass rate.

**New switch at the top:** **Open to students** / **Locked**. Lock a quiz while you are still writing it, or to close it after the deadline.

![The Settings tab](manual:assessment-settings-tab.png)

**Good to know:** when you click **Save changes**, the new settings apply to **new** attempts. Students who already started keep the questions they were given.

Teaching → **Assessments** in the sidebar lists every assessment in your courses.
