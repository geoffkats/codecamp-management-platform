**What changed:** it is now obvious how to edit a lesson or check it as students see it.

- In the course outline, every lesson row has an **Edit** button and an **Open** button on the right.
- **Edit** opens the lesson editor. The editor now uses the full width of the screen; click **Course** at the top to go back to the outline.
- **Open** shows the lesson as students see it, in a new tab, so the builder stays where you left it. In the lesson editor, **Open lesson** at the top does the same.
- The module list on the left no longer goes blank after you open a lesson and come back.
