**What changed:** the Questions tab has a new **random pool** option.

**How to turn it on:**

1. Open the assessment and stay on the **Questions** tab. You need at least two questions.
2. In the random pool panel, choose how many questions each student gets.
3. Click **Use random pool**.

Each student now gets a different random selection, in a different order. Students sitting next to each other won't have the same paper, and a retake gives a fresh set.

**To go back:** click **Use all questions**.

**Scoring:** each student's percentage is based on the points of the questions *they* received, so the score stays fair.
