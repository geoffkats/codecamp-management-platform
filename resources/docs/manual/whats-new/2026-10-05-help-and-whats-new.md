**What changed:** there is now a **Help & What's new** tab on the right edge of every staff page.

**How to use it:**

1. Click the orange tab. The manual slides in from the right.
2. **What's new** (this list) explains each change, when it happened and who it affects.
3. **Guides** walk through each task step by step, with pictures.
4. Search for words like *quiz*, *grading*, *attendance* or *Scratch*.

**The green dot:** when a new change is added, a green dot appears on the tab. It goes away once you've opened What's new.

You only see guides and changes that apply to your role.
