**What changed:** daily reports, club session reports, student submissions and revised content now have a **Comments** section at the bottom.

**Trainers and facilitators:** when a supervisor comments on your report you get a notification. Find your reports under **Daily Reports → My recent reports** (Code Camp) or **My Session Reports** (Code Club). Reports with comments show a speech-bubble count.

**Admins and supervisors:** open a report from **Reports → Daily Reports** (click **View**) or **Reports → Club Session Reports** (click the report), type your feedback and click **Post comment**. Club session reports now open on their own page, where you can also **Mark reviewed**.
