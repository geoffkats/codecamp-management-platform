**What changed:** after submitting, students see:

1. A big **score ring** with their percentage and **Passed!**, **Not passed yet**, or **Waiting for your teacher** (when you still need to mark something).
2. Tiles for **Correct**, **Wrong** and **Points**, and a bar showing the pass mark.
3. **Let's fix these**: every wrong question with *Your answer* in red next to the *Correct answer* in green.
4. **You got these right**: a short list of the questions they got right.
5. **Retake** (when attempts are left) and **Back to Lesson** buttons at the top.

**For staff:** when you open a student's attempt, the **Grade This Submission** panel is still below the results. Nothing changed in how you grade.

What students may see is still controlled by **Show score right away**, **Show correct answers** and **Allow review** in the assessment's Settings tab.
