**What changed:** when a student starts a quiz, the system saves a copy of their questions, the question order and the answer order.

**What this means for you:**

- If you fix a typo or change the correct answer, students **already taking** the quiz are not affected, and **past results don't change**.
- New attempts get the updated question.
- The review and grading pages always show exactly what the student saw, even if the question was later edited, archived or removed.
- A student who refreshes or reconnects gets the same questions back, in the same order.
