# How to Lock/Unlock Quizzes and Assignments

## Quick Guide

### Method 1: From Curriculum Builder (Recommended)

#### For Quizzes/Assessments:

1. **Go to Curriculum Builder**
   - Navigate to: **Curriculum → Builder**
   - Select your course

2. **Click on the Quiz/Assessment**
   - In the left sidebar, click on any quiz/assessment under a lesson
   - The right panel will open showing "Assessment Builder"

3. **Use the Lock/Unlock Button**
   - You'll see a BIG button at the top:
     - **🔒 Red Button** = "Locked - Click to Unlock"
     - **🔓 Green Button** = "Unlocked - Click to Lock"
   - Click the button to toggle
   - Status updates instantly!

4. **Visual Feedback**
   - Below the button, you'll see:
     - ⚠️ "Students cannot access this assessment" (if locked)
     - ✅ "Students can access this assessment" (if unlocked)

#### For Lessons:

1. In the curriculum builder sidebar
2. Hover over any lesson name
3. Click the lock icon that appears on the right
4. Red = Locked, Green = Unlocked

### Method 2: From Assignment Edit Page

1. **Go to the Assignment**
   - Navigate to the assignment you want to lock/unlock

2. **Click Edit**
   - Open the assignment edit page

3. **Use the Lock/Unlock Button**
   - At the top of the page, you'll see "Student Access Control"
   - Big button on the right:
     - **Green Button** = "Unlock Assignment" (currently locked)
     - **Red Button** = "Lock Assignment" (currently unlocked)
   - Click to toggle

### Method 3: Hover in Sidebar (Quick Toggle)

1. In curriculum builder sidebar
2. Hover over any quiz/assessment name
3. Small lock icon appears on the right
4. Click to toggle (Red = Locked, Green = Unlocked)

## Default States

- **Lessons**: Unlocked by default
- **Quizzes/Assessments**: Unlocked by default
- **Assignments**: **LOCKED by default** ⚠️

## What Students See

### When Locked:
- **Quiz**: Cannot click on it, shows lock icon
- **Assignment**: "🔒 Locked - Wait for instructor" message
- **Lesson**: Full "Lesson Locked" page

### When Unlocked:
- Full access to all content
- Can view, complete, and submit

## Tips

✅ **Lock everything at the start** of a course
✅ **Unlock progressively** as students advance
✅ **Use the big buttons** in the assessment panel - they're easier to see
✅ **Check the status message** below the button to confirm
✅ Changes are **instant** - no need to save

## Troubleshooting

**Q: I don't see the lock button**
- Make sure you're in the Curriculum Builder (not regular course view)
- Click on the assessment to open the panel
- The big lock/unlock button should be at the top

**Q: Button doesn't work**
- Check if you have instructor/admin permissions
- Refresh the page and try again
- Check browser console for errors

**Q: Students still see locked content**
- Have them refresh their page
- Check if they're viewing a cached version
- Verify the lock status in curriculum builder
